# Research: tower/dungeon manhwa systems survey
*2026-08-03. Per-series mechanics worth translating. Sources: series fandom
wikis, namu.wiki, CBR explainers; full URL list at the end.*

## Tower of God
- Floor tests are compulsory SETS run by named Ranker administrators; fail
  one test of five and you retake the floor. Same floor, different admin,
  different feel.
- Two-tier identity: letter grade within your cohort (F to A) plus a
  permanent global numeric rank earned only at the top. "Strongest E-rank"
  is itself a marketed identity.
- Positions (Fisherman, Spear Bearer, Light Bearer, Scout, Wave Controller,
  Defender) are battlefield FUNCTIONS, and tests force mixed-position teams.
  Intel/support roles are mandatory, not optional.
- Factions redefine your climb: FUG sponsors chosen climbers with trainers
  and items at the price of servitude; Wolhaiksong wants OUT of the tower.
- Workshop Battle: spectators bet; some contestants play as Gamblers whose
  wallet IS their tournament life. Multi-stage events where stage-1 winnings
  are stage-2 resources.
- Timescale: record ascents take 50 years; floors are countries people live
  in. The climb is a career, not a campaign.
- What fills time between pushes: training school arcs, betting tournaments,
  the Hell Train's themed stations, faction politics, gear brokering.
- Why walls feel like mountains: floors have reputations decades in advance;
  the summit population is visible (named High Rankers).

## Solo Leveling
- Daily quest with a PENALTY INSTANCE for skipping (4-hour survival zone)
  and overflow effort converting into dungeon keys.
- Keys mint private instanced dungeons, a parallel repeatable content track.
- Job Change Quest: a locked-in, no-retreat trial gauntlet that rebirths
  your class identity, gated on level 40.
- "Arise": defeated bosses become recruitable, leveling, formation-saved
  units. The bestiary is the gacha banner.
- Gate economy: timers, gate breaks, mana-crystal mining squads, guild
  monopolies, open-market loot.

## Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint
- Scenario taxonomy: numbered Main spine + optional Subs + condition-locked
  Hiddens + player-vs-player Bounties.
- Constellations (patrons) watch runs through broadcast channels, donate
  coins for STYLE mid-fight, and offer exclusive sponsor contracts that
  graft a second kit plus an agenda.
- One fungible coin wallet: stats, shop, and social actions compete.
- Attributes/stigmas minted by deeds ("achievements-as-abilities" with
  flavor text naming the deed).
- Stories/Fables: accumulated legends are literal power that grows the more
  widely they are known. Reputation is a stat.

## The Tutorial Is Too Hard
- Difficulty select at entry with wildly asymmetric populations; Hell is
  solo by design.
- 100 fixed-pattern floors with safe residential floors at 30/60/90.
  Pattern knowledge is transferable wealth.
- YEARS stuck below one boss; the response is horizontal mastery (technique
  refinement, stat efficiency, companion-raising) that later converts into
  wall-breaking power. The between-time IS the content.
- Cross-difficulty community board; veterans watch rookies' rounds.

## Dungeon Reset
- A daily-resetting dungeon where the protagonist's built infrastructure
  and knowledge persist across resets: farming loop + base persistence.
- Non-combat skills (dismantle, trapcraft) compound into the strongest
  tools. Deliberate death as an information-gathering tool; a codex fills
  in per attempt.

## The Second Coming of Gluttony
- Contribution points earned by serving the world buy levels AND shop
  wishes from the same wallet; a mid-ladder mandatory patron-deity choice
  forks the build.
- Expedition contracts from a hub city with organizer rank requirements.
- Nine Eyes: probabilistic color-graded appraisal, tutorialized through
  gambling.

## SAO (Aincrad) and The Gamer, briefly
- SAO: a self-selected front-liner caste pushes the frontier while most
  players live settler lives; labyrinth mapping data is sold; floor-boss
  raids are scheduled social events with pre-raid strategy councils.
- The Gamer: open-anywhere private grind instances; skills level by literal
  repetition; skill books as learning items.

## Shortlist (most game-translatable)
1. Authored floor-test sets with retake rights (ToG)
2. Cohort letter grade + permanent global rank (ToG)
3. Content that checks team ROLES, not just damage (ToG positions)
4. Faction sponsorship that redefines the win condition (ToG)
5. Penalty-instance dailies + overflow-to-keys (SL)
6. Boss-to-collection pipeline (SL "Arise")
7. Lock-in class-rebirth trial (SL job change)
8. Patron/audience economy paying for style (ORV)
9. Main/sub/hidden scenario taxonomy (ORV)
10. Deeds-as-abilities, stories-as-prestige (ORV)
11. Fixed-pattern floors + horizontal mastery as wall fuel (Tutorial)
12. Persistent homestead inside a resetting dungeon (Dungeon Reset)
