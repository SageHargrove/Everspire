Parallax hero cards

Move the pointer across a card. The framed art tilts and its layers separate; the name and stars below stay flat, so the card reads as a page with a window cut into it rather than a slab being skewed. Max deflection is 6° and layer travel is 6px — deliberately restrained. The styles below the divider are sliced straight out of src/index.css at build time, so this is the shipping effect and not an approximation. Turn on your OS “reduce motion” setting and it all goes flat.
