THE TOWER

Played from an illuminated war-table, and every screen below is the real game. Summon a company. Chart the ascent. Watch the fray.

Chart the Ascent

One hundred floors across ten zones, and every floor is dealt fresh the moment you reach it. Scout ahead, read the hazards, and decide which company takes the stairs.

Turn the Cards

Summons arrive face-down. Flip them one at a time or all at once, and meet whoever answered the Gate.

Watch the Fray

You choose the team, the leader, and the formation. The fight runs itself, and the Chronicle writes down every swing. Managers plan. Heroes fight.

Keep the Company

Every hero keeps their own stats, story, morale, and opinion of you. Bring them home and they remember it. Lose them and everyone else remembers too.

Build Between Climbs

The base is the other half of the game. Craft, research, cook, and heal, and put the heroes who cannot fight to work on the ones who can.

Floor 40
HOW IT PLAYS

Giltgrave is a strategy game in a gacha's clothes. The Tower cannot be paid off. It can only be understood.

The Tower shows its hand once

A floor's hazards, elites, and boss phases are fixed the moment it is dealt. Scout it, lose to it, learn it. A climber who reads the floor beats one who out-levels it.

Talent outranks rarity

Every hero rolls hidden aptitudes, and a gifted low-star can out-grow a lazy six-star. The stars say where a hero started, not where they can end up. Write nobody off.

Choose your guarantee

Summoning builds Sparks. Spend them and the next 5-star is guaranteed to be a class you picked. Bad luck runs out; planning does not.

The Tower checks your bench

Deeper floors require several full teams, and tired heroes sit out. The winning roster is built wide, with replacements trained before they are needed.
