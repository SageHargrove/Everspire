Everspire

A roguelike tower-climbing gacha RPG. Your heroes die, and they stay dead.

Download for Windows All versions

Free playtest build · Windows 10/11 · ~1 GB. Not code-signed yet, so Windows will show a “protected your PC” warning on first launch — click More info → Run anyway.

What it is

  • Permadeath with a memory. Fallen heroes leave Deeds, Legacies, and grieving squadmates behind.
  • A tower that fights back. Zone hazards, elite affixes, boss phases, and floors that punish a bad formation.
  • Heroes with opinions. Egos, bonds, mentors, morale — a hero who resents your lineup will act on it.
  • A base worth building. Forge, athenaeum, tavern, research, expeditions.

Optional extras

Your own hero art

With an NVIDIA GPU, turn on portrait generation in Settings and the game downloads its own image pipeline. Every hero you roll is one nobody else will ever have. Skip it and everyone shares the same art.

Written-for-you heroes

Paste an Anthropic API key into Settings and names, backstories and banter get written for your specific hero. Without one you get a large pool of pre-written text and every system still works.
